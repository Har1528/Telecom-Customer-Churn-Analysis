# Images

This folder contains the visualizations generated during the exploratory data analysis (EDA) and model evaluation stages of the project.

## Contents

- **churn_distribution.png** – Overall distribution of churned and retained customers.
- **contract_churn.png** – Churn comparison across different contract types.
- **tenure_histogram.png** – Distribution of customer tenure.
- **monthly_charge_histogram.png** – Distribution of monthly charges.
- **feature_importance.png** – Top features identified by the Random Forest model.
- **roc_curve.png** – ROC curve of the final Logistic Regression model.
- **confusion_matrix.png** – Confusion matrix of the final Logistic Regression model.

These visualizations support the business insights and machine learning results presented in the notebook and README.
